## Hello ! Bonjour !

I am Eulalie Delaune. 

I am a time series methodologist. I work with [Anna Smyk](https://github.com/annasmyk) on Time Series Analysis, especially Seasonal Adjustment at Insee, in France, and mainly on [JDemetra+](https://github.com/jdemetra) software. I am a part of the [COSA project](https://cosa-grant.org/).

My role is to maintain, improve and document these R packages linked to JDemetra+:

- [rjd3jars](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3jars) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3jars)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3jars)
- [rjd3toolkit](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3toolkit) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3toolkit)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3toolkit)
- [rjd3x13](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3x13) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3x13)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3x13)
- [rjd3tramoseats](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3tramoseats) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3tramoseats)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3tramoseats)
- [rjd3providers](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3providers) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3providers)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3providers)
- [rjd3workspace](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3workspace) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3workspace)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3workspace)
- [rjd3production](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3production) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3production)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3production)
- [rjd3qr](https://github.com/rjdverse/rjd3qr) [![CRAN status](https://www.r-pkg.org/badges/version/rjd3qr)](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rjd3qr)

I also work on the organization of the next edition of Use of R in Official Statistics (uRos), Nov 18-20 2026, Paris ([website here](https://r-project.ro/conference2026.html)).

I am interested in Singular Spectrum Analysis for Time Series, and work on a new variant of the method, called CASSAVA (Complete Adaptive Singular Spectrum Analysis for Variation Adjustment).
